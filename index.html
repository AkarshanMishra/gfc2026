At Grillista, we believe food is more than just a meal — it's a reason to bring people together. Founded in 2021 with our first outlet in Kakadeo, Kanpur, we started with a simple idea: to serve great food in a warm, welcoming space.

What began as a single outlet has now grown to two company-owned outlets in Kanpur, where we have proudly served 10,000+ happy customers. Our journey is driven by a passion for good food, great service, and memorable experiences.
