ASKED QUESTIONS

★

Find answers to the most common questions about Grillista Food Franchise.

Q

What is Grillista Food Franchise?

−

Grillista is a fast-growing food brand known for its delicious food, great ambience, and excellent customer service. We offer a profitable franchise opportunity with complete end-to-end support.

Q

What are the requirements to become a franchisee?

+

You need a commercial space of 150 to 500+ sq.ft. (depending on the model: Kiosk, Cafe, or Dine-In), adequate investment capability, and passion for the food & hospitality industry. No prior restaurant experience is mandatory as our team provides complete training.

Q

How much investment is required?

+

Investment packages start from ₹3.5 Lakhs for Kiosk models, ₹5.5 Lakhs for Cafe & Takeaway, and ₹8.5 Lakhs+ for full Dine-In models. This covers equipment, signage, initial setup, staff training, and raw material support.

Q

What support will I get after taking the franchise?

+

Grillista provides comprehensive 360° support: location selection, kitchen design, chef and staff recruitment & training, centralized recipe ingredients, Zomato/Swiggy onboarding, and digital marketing campaigns.

Q

How long does it take to set up an outlet?

+

From agreement signing and site finalization to grand opening, the entire setup typically takes just 15 to 30 days.

Q

What is the expected ROI?

+

With healthy operating profit margins of 35% to 45% and strong customer retention, most Grillista partners achieve full capital payback within 6 to 12 months.
