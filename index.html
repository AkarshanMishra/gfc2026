FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

QUESTIONS YOU MIGHT ASK

What is Grillista?

Grillista is a high-growth, 100% vegetarian Indian fast-casual QSR brand offering handcrafted burgers, loaded pizzas, garlic breads, desi street bites, pastas, coolers, and thick shakes engineered for Indian tastebuds.

Is Grillista 100% Vegetarian?

Yes, Grillista is 100% pure vegetarian with dedicated kitchen protocols, premium paneer, farm-fresh vegetables, and high-protein alternatives.

Where was Grillista founded?

Grillista was founded in 2021 in Kakadeo, Kanpur, and expanded with company-owned outlets in Barra, Kanpur, serving over 10,000+ satisfied customers before launching its nationwide franchise program.

How long does it take to set up a Grillista outlet?

From site sign-off and agreement execution to grand opening, it takes approximately 30 to 45 days for Express/Bistro models and 45 to 60 days for Signature flagship restaurants.

Does Grillista provide turnkey store design and kitchen setup?

Yes, our central projects team delivers full 3D architectural CAD layout, MEP design, certified kitchen equipment procurement, cloud POS integration, and outdoor 3D brand signage fabrication.

What are the carpet area requirements for different models?

Express Model: 100 – 250 sq.ft (Kiosk / Food Court / Takeaway)
Bistro Model: 250 – 500 sq.ft (High Street / Quick Dine-In)
Signature Model: 500 – 1,200 sq.ft (Flagship High-Street / Full Dine-In Lounge)

Does Grillista help in location scouting and feasibility audits?

Yes, our dedicated real estate team conducts on-ground footfall audits, vehicular density analysis, competitor benchmarking, and rental viability studies before greenlighting any location.

Does Grillista help in hiring and kitchen staff training?

Yes, Grillista provides complete chef and staff recruitment support, followed by a mandatory 14-day training certification covering kitchen SOPs, speed-of-service, food hygiene, inventory management, and POS billing.

Do I need prior restaurant experience to run a franchise?

No prior restaurant experience is required! Our simplified kitchen automation, standardized central sauces and seasonings, and foolproof operating SOPs make running an outlet hassle-free.

What marketing support does Grillista provide?

We execute multi-channel marketing: hyper-local Meta/Instagram ad campaigns, Zomato & Swiggy algorithm optimization, influencer tasting sessions, PR media releases, launch discount collateral, and seasonal festive menu promotions.

What is the expected Net Profit across different formats?

Express Model: Net Profit ~₹45,750 / month (~18% Net Margin)
Bistro Model: Net Profit ~₹1,45,200 / month (~35% Net Margin)
Signature Model: Net Profit ~₹2,20,000 / month (~36% Net Margin)

What is the food cost and royalty structure?

Standardized food cost is kept low at ~30% via centralized bulk supply. Royalty is 0% (Free for first 6 months) and 5% monthly gross sales thereafter.

What is the franchise agreement tenure?

The standard franchise agreement is signed for a 5-year term and is renewable at zero renewal fee subject to compliant brand operations.

Is the franchise location protected with territory exclusivity?

Yes, each franchise outlet is granted an exclusive catchment radius to ensure maximum customer concentration without internal cannibalization.
