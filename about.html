ABOUT US

THE IDEA

Founded in 2021, Grillista began its journey with its first company-owned outlet in Kakadeo, Kanpur. In 2024, we expanded to our second company-owned outlet in Barra, Kanpur. Across both locations, we have served 10,000+ customers, gaining valuable experience across customer preferences, menu development, pricing and day-to-day restaurant operations.

Our company-owned journey has been a deliberate step towards building a stronger and more sustainable business model. By operating, learning and refining the concept ourselves, we have developed practical insights into what works in the market before inviting franchise partners to join us. This experience gives our partners the confidence of building on a model shaped by real-world learning, rather than an untested concept.
